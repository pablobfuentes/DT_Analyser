DT Analyser — how to use

1. Double-click DT_Analyser.exe
2. A black window opens. Leave it open.
3. Your browser should open the app by itself.
   If not, go to:  http://127.0.0.1:8765
4. When you are done, close the black window.

Your trades are saved on this computer only
(folder:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\LocalTraderAnalyzer).

Windows may warn that the app is unknown. Choose
"More info" → "Run anyway" if that happens.
